Sambal Goreng Kentang Ati (Spicy Potatoes with Liver)

★★★★☆ 4.5 (90 reviews)
⏱ Prep: 15m | Cook: 30m | Total: 45m 👥 Servings: 6 servings 📊 Difficulty: Medium 🍽️ Category: Snacks & Appetizers

Sambal Goreng Kentang Ati (Spicy Potatoes with Liver) is a beloved Indonesian dish that brings together the rich flavors of traditional spices and fresh ingredients. This authentic recipe guides you through every step, from selecting the right ingredients to achieving the perfect texture and taste. Whether you're a seasoned cook or trying Indonesian cuisine for the first time, this recipe delivers restaurant-quality results right in your own kitchen.

Sambal Goreng Kentang Ati (Spicy Potatoes with Liver) represents the heart of Indonesian home cooking — flavorful, aromatic, and made with love. This step-by-step guide ensures perfect results every time, whether you're cooking for family, friends, or just yourself.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 500 g potatoes cut diced
  • 250 g hliver chicken sapi rebus
  • 5 tbsp thick coconut milk
  • 10 red chilies
  • 6 shallots
  • 4 garlic
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 cm galangal
  • Salt and sugar to taste
  • Minyak untuk menggoreng

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Deep fry kentang until cooked, Set aside.
  2. Grind into a paste cabai, shallots, garlic.
  3. Sauté spice paste with bay leaves and galangal until fragrant.
  4. Add hati, Mix well.
  5. Add coconut milk, salt, and gula. Cook until kuah thickens.
  6. Add kentang Deep fry, Mix well. Serve.

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • • Use fresh, high-quality ingredients for the best flavor.
  • • Adjust seasoning to your taste — traditional Indonesian cooking is all about balance.
  • • Prep all ingredients before you start cooking (mise en place) for a smooth workflow.

🔄 Variations

  • Protein swap — substitute the main protein with chicken, tofu, or tempeh
  • Spice level — adjust chili quantity for mild or extra spicy version
  • Dietary — make it vegan by omitting animal products and using plant-based alternatives

🍚 What to Serve With

This dish pairs perfectly with:

  • Steamed jasmine rice or coconut rice (nasi uduk)
  • Kerupuk (Indonesian shrimp crackers) for crunch
  • Sambal terasi or sambal matah for extra heat
  • Fresh cucumber and tomato slices as a refreshing side
  • Try serving with Mie Goreng Jawa (Javanese Fried Noodles)

Can I make this dish ahead of time?

Yes! Many Indonesian dishes actually taste better the next day as the flavors continue to develop. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.
