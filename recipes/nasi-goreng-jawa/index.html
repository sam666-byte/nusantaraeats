Nasi Goreng (Indonesian Fried Rice)

★★★★☆ 4.8 (96 reviews)
⏱ Prep: 10m | Cook: 20m | Total: 30m 👥 Servings: 2 servings 📊 Difficulty: Easy 🍽️ Category: Main Dishes

Nasi Goreng (Indonesian Fried Rice) is a beloved Indonesian dish that brings together the rich flavors of traditional spices and fresh ingredients. This authentic recipe guides you through every step, from selecting the right ingredients to achieving the perfect texture and taste. Whether you're a seasoned cook or trying Indonesian cuisine for the first time, this recipe delivers restaurant-quality results right in your own kitchen.

Nasi Goreng (Indonesian Fried Rice) represents the heart of Indonesian home cooking — flavorful, aromatic, and made with love. This step-by-step guide ensures perfect results every time, whether you're cooking for family, friends, or just yourself.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 2 plates of white cold rice
  • 2 garlic minced
  • 3 shallots sliced
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp sweet soy sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • Coconut oil to taste
  • Garnish: crackers, pickles, tomato, green onions

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Heat oil, Sauté shallots and garlic until fragrant.
  2. Set aside to the side of the wok, Add eggs, scrambled until cooked.
  3. Add rice, Mix well with High heat.
  4. Add sweet soy sauce, salt, pepper. Stir quickly until evenly mixed.
  5. Cook 3-5 minutes until rice slightly dry.
  6. Serve warm with crackers, pickles, and slices tomato.

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • • Use day-old cold rice for the best texture — freshly cooked rice turns mushy.
  • • Cook on high heat (wok hei) for that authentic smoky flavor.
  • • Add the kecap manis at the end to prevent burning.

🔄 Variations

  • Chicken Nasi Goreng — add diced chicken breast
  • Seafood Nasi Goreng — add shrimp and squid
  • Vegetarian Nasi Goreng — replace egg with tofu

🍚 What to Serve With

This dish pairs perfectly with:

  • Steamed jasmine rice or coconut rice (nasi uduk)
  • Kerupuk (Indonesian shrimp crackers) for crunch
  • Sambal terasi or sambal matah for extra heat
  • Fresh cucumber and tomato slices as a refreshing side
