Instructions

  1. Grind into a paste garlic, turmeric, ginger, coriander, salt.

  2. Coat the fish with spice paste, soy sauce, and lime juice. Let rest for 30 minutes.

  3. Grill the fish over hot coals while brushing with the remaining spice paste.

  4. Flip the fish, grill until both sides are cooked.

  5. Serve with sambal matah and warm rice.

Tips for Best Results

  • Use fresh, high-quality ingredients for the best flavor.
  • Adjust seasoning to your taste — traditional Indonesian cooking is all about balance.
  • Prep all ingredients before you start cooking (mise en place) for a smooth workflow.
