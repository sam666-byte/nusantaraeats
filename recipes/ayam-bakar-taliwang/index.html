Ayam Taliwang (Lombok Grilled Chicken)

★★★★☆ 4.7 (94 reviews)
⏱ Prep: 30m | Cook: 60m | Total: 90m 👥 Servings: 4 servings 📊 Difficulty: Medium 🍽️ Category: Grilled & Satay

Ayam Taliwang (Lombok Grilled Chicken) is a beloved Indonesian dish that brings together the rich flavors of traditional spices and fresh ingredients. This authentic recipe guides you through every step, from selecting the right ingredients to achieving the perfect texture and taste. Whether you're a seasoned cook or trying Indonesian cuisine for the first time, this recipe delivers restaurant-quality results right in your own kitchen.

Ayam Taliwang (Lombok Grilled Chicken) represents the heart of Indonesian home cooking — flavorful, aromatic, and made with love. This step-by-step guide ensures perfect results every time, whether you're cooking for family, friends, or just yourself.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1 whole free-range chicken
  • 15 red chilies
  • 10 bird eye chilies
  • 8 shallots
  • 5 garlic
  • 2 cm aromatic ginger
  • 2 candlenuts
  • 1 tsp shrimp paste bakar
  • 200 ml coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp air asam
  • Salt and sugar merah

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Grind into a paste semua spice paste. Sauté until fragrant.
  2. Add coconut milk, air asam, salt, gula. Cook until mendidih.
  3. Add ayam, simmer over low heat until bumbu meresap.
  4. Grill ayam while diBrush sisa bumbu until golden brown.
  5. Serve with warm rice and plecing kangkung.

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • • Use fresh, high-quality ingredients for the best flavor.
  • • Adjust seasoning to your taste — traditional Indonesian cooking is all about balance.
  • • Prep all ingredients before you start cooking (mise en place) for a smooth workflow.

🔄 Variations

  • Protein swap — substitute the main protein with chicken, tofu, or tempeh
  • Spice level — adjust chili quantity for mild or extra spicy version
  • Dietary — make it vegan by omitting animal products and using plant-based alternatives

🍚 What to Serve With

This dish pairs perfectly with:

  • Steamed jasmine rice or coconut rice (nasi uduk)
  • Kerupuk (Indonesian shrimp crackers) for crunch
  • Sambal terasi or sambal matah for extra heat
  • Fresh cucumber and tomato slices as a refreshing side
  • Try serving with Nasi Liwet Solo (Solo-Style Coconut Rice)

What is Ayam Taliwang (Lombok Grilled Chicken)?

Ayam Taliwang (Lombok Grilled Chicken) is a traditional Indonesian dish made with authentic spices and fresh ingredients. It's known for its rich flavor and cultural significance in Indonesian cuisine.

How long does it take to make Ayam Taliwang (Lombok Grilled Chicken)?

The total time is approximately 90 minutes, including preparation and cooking time.

How many servings does this recipe make?

This recipe yields 4 orang.

Can I make this dish ahead of time?

Yes! Many Indonesian dishes actually taste better the next day as the flavors continue to develop. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.

What do you serve with Ayam Taliwang (Lombok Grilled Chicken)?

This dish pairs beautifully with steamed jasmine rice, kerupuk (Indonesian crackers), and sambal (chili sauce).

Is this dish spicy?

The spice level is adjustable. You can reduce or increase the chili according to your preference.

Can I make this easier or vegetarian?

You can adapt this recipe by substituting protein with tofu or tempeh. Adjust cooking times accordingly.
