Ayam Goreng Lengkuas (Galangal Fried Chicken)

★★★★☆ 4.6 (92 reviews)
⏱ Prep: 20m | Cook: 40m | Total: 60m 👥 Servings: 4 servings 📊 Difficulty: Easy 🍽️ Category: Main Dishes

Ayam Goreng Lengkuas (Galangal Fried Chicken) is a beloved Indonesian dish that brings together the rich flavors of traditional spices and fresh ingredients. This authentic recipe guides you through every step, from selecting the right ingredients to achieving the perfect texture and taste. Whether you're a seasoned cook or trying Indonesian cuisine for the first time, this recipe delivers restaurant-quality results right in your own kitchen.

Ayam Goreng Lengkuas (Galangal Fried Chicken) represents the heart of Indonesian home cooking — flavorful, aromatic, and made with love. This step-by-step guide ensures perfect results every time, whether you're cooking for family, friends, or just yourself.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken cut 8
  • 3 cm galangal parut
  • 5 garlic
  • 3 cm turmeric
  • 2 cm ginger
  • 1 tsp coriander
  • Salt and bouillon powder
  • Air untuk merebus
  • Minyak untuk menggoreng

👨‍🍳 Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Grind into a paste garlic, turmeric, ginger, coriander, salt.
  2. Coat ayam with spice paste and galangal parut.
  3. Boil ayam with bumbu and sedikit water until tender and water habis.
  4. Heat oil, Deep fry ayam until golden brown.
  5. Deep fry juga sisa bumbu galangal until dry untuk taburan.
  6. Serve with warm rice and sambal.

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • • Use fresh, high-quality ingredients for the best flavor.
  • • Adjust seasoning to your taste — traditional Indonesian cooking is all about balance.
  • • Prep all ingredients before you start cooking (mise en place) for a smooth workflow.

🔄 Variations

  • Protein swap — substitute the main protein with chicken, tofu, or tempeh
  • Spice level — adjust chili quantity for mild or extra spicy version
  • Dietary — make it vegan by omitting animal products and using plant-based alternatives

🍚 What to Serve With

This dish pairs perfectly with:

  • Steamed jasmine rice or coconut rice (nasi uduk)
  • Kerupuk (Indonesian shrimp crackers) for crunch
  • Sambal terasi or sambal matah for extra heat
  • Fresh cucumber and tomato slices as a refreshing side
  • Try serving with Es Teler (Indonesian Mixed Fruit Drink)

Can I make this dish ahead of time?

Yes! Many Indonesian dishes actually taste better the next day as the flavors continue to develop. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.

The spice level is adjustable. You can reduce or increase the chili according to your preference.

Can I make this vegetarian?
